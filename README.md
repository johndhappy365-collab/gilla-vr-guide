# Gilla VR Guide

An unofficial fan guide to Gilla VR, the free multiplayer VR horror game for Meta Quest.

Read the guide: https://gillavr.co

## What is Gilla VR?

Gilla VR (GILLA) is a free multiplayer VR horror game by Play Gilla, available only on Meta Quest (Quest 2, 3, 3S and Pro). You move with gorilla-style arm swinging, no joystick walking, and the monster can hear your voice. It is free to play: the in-game shop only sells cosmetics and bundles; every mode is free.

The official game is at https://gillavr.com and on the Meta Horizon Store. This guide is not affiliated with Play Gilla or Meta.

## What the guide covers

- How to play: your first hour, controls, and the three modes
- The Base: collect gold bars and diamonds, feed the recycler, use keycards
- Be the Monster: one player hunts the rest for 2 minutes, with a sonar ping every 45 seconds
- Don't Splash: platforms sink under you while Mako hunts from the water
- The three monsters: Grinner, Mannequin and Mako
- The caves, sandbox mode, room codes, and the official Discord server
- A free Be the Monster hunt timer that runs in your browser

The full guide (22 pages) is at https://gillavr.co — free to read, no sign-up.
